About Elite Glo

Young Entrepreneurs, Professional Results

Elite Glo was founded by a group of motivated teenagers who turned their passion for cars into a professional car washing and detailing business. What started as a summer job idea has grown into a trusted service in the Fremont community.

Despite our young age, we're committed to delivering professional-quality results. We use eco-friendly products and proven techniques to ensure your car gets the care it deserves.

Our mission is simple: provide excellent car care services while building valuable business experience and serving our community with enthusiasm and dedication.
